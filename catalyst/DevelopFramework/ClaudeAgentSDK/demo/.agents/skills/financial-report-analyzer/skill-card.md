## Description: <br>
Analyze financial reports and research notes (PDF, Word, images) to extract key financial metrics, profitability trends, risk signals, and management commentary. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[soul-code](https://clawhub.ai/user/soul-code) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, analysts, and finance teams use this skill to parse financial reports or research notes with SoMark, then produce structured AI-assisted financial metric, trend, risk, and management-commentary analysis for due diligence or reporting. The analysis is informational only and not investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global; users choose the appropriate SoMark endpoint for mainland China or outside mainland China. <br>
